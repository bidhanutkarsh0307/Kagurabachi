THE STORY OF

— KAGURABACHI —

Chihiro Rokuhira's father was the greatest swordsmith alive — until the Sinister Sorcerers murdered him and stole his six enchanted blades, weapons of catastrophic power.

Now Chihiro wields the one blade his father left him and hunts down every last sorcerer to reclaim what was taken — leaving a trail of blood and ash in his wake.
